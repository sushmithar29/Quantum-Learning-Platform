Foundation Concept

The Bloch Sphere Geometry

Every pure single-qubit quantum state can be uniquely represented as a point on the surface of a unit sphere in 3D Euclidean space:

|ψ⟩ = \cos(\theta/2)|0⟩ + e^{i\phi}\sin(\theta/2)|1⟩
Current State Coordinates:
Ket: |0⟩
θ: 0° · φ: 0°
Apply Unitary Gate Rotations:
🎓 Key Takeaways:
  • North Pole: State $|0\rangle$ ($\theta = 0$). South Pole: State $|1\rangle$ ($\theta = \pi$).
  • Equator: Equal superposition states ($|\alpha|^2 = |\beta|^2 = 0.5$). State $|+\rangle$ is at $+X$, $|-\rangle$ is at $-X$.
  • Quantum Gates as Rotations: All single-qubit gates are rigid rotations $R_{\hat{n}}(\alpha)$ around an axis $\hat{n}$ on the sphere.
