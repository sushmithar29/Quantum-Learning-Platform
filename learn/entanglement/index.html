What is Entanglement?

When two qubits interact, they can form an entangled state that cannot be factored into individual single-qubit states $|ψ_A⟩ \otimes |ψ_B⟩$. Measuring one qubit instantly determines the outcome of the other, regardless of spatial separation.

|Φ⁺⟩ = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(|00⟩ + |11⟩)
🎓 Key Takeaways:
  • Neither qubit possesses an independent definite state prior to measurement.
  • In state $|Φ^+\rangle$, measuring Qubit 0 as 0 guarantees Qubit 1 collapses to 0 with 100% correlation.
  • Entanglement is the core physical resource enabling quantum teleportation, superdense coding, and quantum cryptography (QKD).

💡 Non-Separability
Entangled states violate Bell's inequalities, mathematically disproving local hidden variable theories (Nobel Prize in Physics 2022). Quantum mechanics is fundamentally non-local in its correlations.
CNOT Gate: A Hadamard on qubit 0 followed by a Controlled-NOT onto qubit 1 constructs Bell states from |00⟩.
