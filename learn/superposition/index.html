Foundation Concept

Quantum Superposition

Superposition is not merely classical ignorance or probabilistic mixture. A qubit in superposition simultaneously traverses multiple computational paths until measured.

H|0⟩ = |+⟩ = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(|0⟩ + |1⟩)
🎓 Key Takeaways:
  • Applying Hadamard twice restores the initial state: $H(H|0\rangle) = |0\rangle$ due to constructive & destructive interference.
  • Relative phase $\phi$ is invisible in direct Z-measurement, but causes interference when rotated.
  • Quantum algorithms (Grover, Shor) use superposition for parallel evaluation and interference to amplify the correct answer.

💡 Why Phase Matters
Both states |+⟩ = (|0⟩ + |1⟩)/√2 and |-⟩ = (|0⟩ - |1⟩)/√2 have 50% probability of yielding 0 or 1. However, passing them through a second beam-splitter / Hadamard gate yields 100% |0⟩ for |+⟩, and 100% |1⟩ for |-⟩.
Interference: Amplitudes can cancel out (destructively) or amplify (constructively), enabling quantum speedups.
