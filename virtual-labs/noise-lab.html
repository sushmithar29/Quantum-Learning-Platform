🎯 Section 01

Experiment Aim

To simulate open quantum system dynamics by applying four canonical quantum noise channels — bit-flip, phase-flip, depolarizing, and amplitude damping — to a qubit prepared in an arbitrary superposition state. The experiment investigates the shrinkage of the Bloch vector from the unit sphere surface toward the mixed-state interior, computes quantum state fidelity F(ρ, ρ₀) = |⟨ψ₀|ρ|ψ₀⟩|², and visualizes the decoherence process in real time.

📖 Section 02

Theoretical Foundations

1. Quantum Noise Channels (Kraus Representation)

Noise in open quantum systems is modeled as a quantum channel ε: ρ → Σ Kᵢ ρ Kᵢ†, where the Kraus operators satisfy Σ Kᵢ†Kᵢ = I.

Bit-Flip: ε_bf(ρ) = (1-p)ρ + p·X·ρ·X
Phase-Flip: ε_pf(ρ) = (1-p)ρ + p·Z·ρ·Z
Depolarizing: ε_dp(ρ) = (1-p)ρ + (p/3)(X·ρ·X + Y·ρ·Y + Z·ρ·Z)
Amplitude Damp: ε_ad(ρ) = K₀·ρ·K₀† + K₁·ρ·K₁†
K₀ = [[1,0],[0,√(1-γ)]], K₁ = [[0,√γ],[0,0]]

2. Decoherence and Bloch Vector Contraction

Decoherence transforms a pure state Bloch vector r (|r|=1) into a mixed state with |r| < 1. The contraction factors differ per channel:

Bit-flip: (x, y, z) → (x, (1-2p)y, (1-2p)z) — shrinks y, z components
Phase-flip: (x, y, z) → ((1-2p)x, (1-2p)y, z) — preserves z, shrinks xy-plane
Depolarizing: (x, y, z) → (1-4p/3)·(x, y, z) — isotropic shrinkage
Amp. Damping: (x, y) → √(1-γ)·(x,y), z → (1-γ)z+γ — energy relaxation T₁

3. Quantum State Fidelity

F(ρ, |ψ₀⟩) = ⟨ψ₀|ρ|ψ₀⟩ ∈ [0, 1]
F = 1: perfect fidelity (pure state, no noise)
F → 0.5: maximally mixed state (complete decoherence)
🎓 Section 03

Learning Objectives

  • Understand Kraus operators and their role in modeling environmental decoherence as a quantum channel.
  • Distinguish T1 (energy relaxation, amplitude damping) from T2 (dephasing, phase flip) noise timescales.
  • Observe the geometric contraction of pure state Bloch vectors into the interior of the Bloch ball under noise.
  • Compute quantum state fidelity F and observe how it degrades as noise strength p increases.
  • Compare the qualitative effects of four distinct noise channels on the density matrix and measurement statistics.

01

02

👁️ Section 06

Observation Analysis

A

Bloch Vector Shrinkage

As noise strength increases, the Bloch vector shrinks from the unit sphere surface (|r|=1, pure state) toward the origin (|r|=0, maximally mixed state). The direction of shrinkage depends on the noise channel type.

B

Phase-Flip vs. Bit-Flip

Phase-flip preserves P(|0⟩) in Z-basis but destroys off-diagonal coherences (shrinks x,y components). Bit-flip changes P(|0⟩) by flipping the |0⟩↔|1⟩ populations (shrinks y,z components).

C

Depolarizing Maximum Noise

At p=25% (for 4-operator depolarizing), the state becomes completely mixed ρ=I/2 — the Bloch vector reaches the origin. All measurement outcomes become 50:50 regardless of initial state.
